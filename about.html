Our Journey Since 1962

History of Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School

Established in 1962 by visionary community elders and educationists in Nongmensong, Shillong, Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School began with a noble mission to bring accessible education to children of the locality and surrounding areas.

Recognized as a Government Aided School (UDISE Code: 17060206809), the institution has steadily grown from its humble beginnings into a full-fledged proceeding secondary school offering classes from Nursery to Class X under the Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE). Generation after generation, our school has remained true to its founding motto: "Service Before Self."
