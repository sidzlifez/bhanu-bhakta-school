History & Mission

Founded in 1962, Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School (UDISE: 17060206809) is a Government Aided educational institution located in Nongmensong, Shillong. For over six decades, the school has championed accessible education from Nursery to Class X under the Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE).

Named in honour of Adikavi Bhanu Bhakta Acharya, our institution blends academic rigour with moral values, cultural heritage, and community service.
