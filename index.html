About Our School

Welcome to Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School

Located in the heart of Nongmensong, Shillong, Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School is committed to providing accessible, high-quality education from Nursery to Class X aligned with the Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE) curriculum.

Guided by our motto, "Service Before Self," our dedicated faculty and Managing Committee work closely with parents to nurture academic excellence, character formation, cultural pride, and community responsibility in every student.

Message to Students & Parents

"Education is not merely the acquisition of facts, but the training of the mind and character to serve society. At Bhanu Bhakta Nepali Proceeding Secondary School, we strive to inspire every child to dream big, work hard, and uphold our guiding principle of Service Before Self."
